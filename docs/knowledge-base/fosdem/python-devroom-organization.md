# Python devroom organization at FOSDEM

!!! note "Draft status"

    Draft compiled from online sources on 2026-09-20. Organizer names are only listed where a
    published source names them; years without a published CfP have no verified organizer list.
    Schedule and video data inventory notes live in the repo at `early-research/fosdem-data-sources.md`.

The Python devroom has run at FOSDEM every year since 2013. The first Sunday or Saturday slot
of the conference is reserved for it, and the main CfP trail lives on the
[FOSDEM mailing list](https://lists.fosdem.org/pipermail/fosdem/) archives.

## Devroom contact points and community infrastructure

- A dedicated devroom mailing list is used for organizing discussions:
  `https://lists.fosdem.org/listinfo/python-devroom`
  ([2015 CfP](https://lists.fosdem.org/pipermail/fosdem/2014-November/002067.html)). The list
  archive is not publicly indexed in 2026 (the address returns a
  404, [lists.fosdem.org](https://lists.fosdem.org/pipermail/python-devroom/)) — the archive is
  private or the list is retired.
- The community site `python-fosdem.org` has carried talk schedules, its own
  CfP pages, and a code of conduct for 2026
  ([python-fosdem.org](https://python-fosdem.org/)).
- A `@PythonFOSDEM` Twitter account is used for announcements
  ([2015 CfP](https://lists.fosdem.org/pipermail/fosdem/2014-November/002067.html)).
- The Belgium Python community list
  (`https://mail.python.org/mailman/listinfo/belgium`) is used for volunteers and local coordination
  ([2020 CfP](https://lists.fosdem.org/pipermail/fosdem/2019q4/002885.html)); organizers point to
  it as the channel to reach them about helping run the devroom.
- A contact mailbox `info@python-fosdem.org` is given in some years for questions
  ([2015 CfP](https://lists.fosdem.org/pipermail/fosdem/2014-November/002067.html)).

### Submission platform history

- Pentabarf (`penta.fosdem.org`) was the submission system through FOSDEM 2022
  ([2017 CfP](https://lists.fosdem.org/pipermail/fosdem/2016-October/002462.html) uses Pentabarf;
  [2021 CfP](https://lists.fosdem.org/pipermail/fosdem/2020q4/003114.html) still uses Pentabarf).
- FOSDEM 2024 switched to self-hosted Pretalx; "Pretalx is a new system, if you already had a
  pentabarf account you will not be able to re-use it"
  ([2024 CfP](https://lists.fosdem.org/pipermail/fosdem/2023q4/003508.html)).
- 2013 and 2014 editions used a survey hosted on python-fosdem.org instead of Pentabarf
  ([2013 CfP](https://pycon.blogspot.com/2012/12/cfp-for-python-room-at-fosdem-2013.html),
  [2014 CfP](https://www.mail-archive.com/python-list@python.org/msg365462.html)).

## Talk format over the years

- **2013** — Talk length not stated in the surviving CfP text; the
  [2013 track page](https://archive.fosdem.org/2013/schedule/track/python/) shows 25-minute slots.
- **2014–2017** — 30 minutes including Q&A
  ([2015 CfP](https://lists.fosdem.org/pipermail/fosdem/2014-November/002067.html),
  [2016 CfP](https://www.mail-archive.com/psf-community%40python.org/msg00003.html),
  [2017 CfP](https://www.mail-archive.com/python-list@python.org/msg421059.html)).
- **2019–2020** — 20 minutes of content plus 5 minutes of questions
  ([2019 CfP](https://lists.fosdem.org/pipermail/fosdem/2018q4/002781.html),
  [2020 CfP](https://lists.fosdem.org/pipermail/fosdem/2019q4/002885.html)).
- **2023** — "Between 15min and 30min of presentation", no planned Q&A
  ([archived CfP](https://web.archive.org/web/20230401125950/https://python-fosdem.org/call-for-proposals/)).
- **2021–2022 and 2024–2026** — 25 minutes of content (plus 5 minutes of questions in 2021–2022)
  ([2021 CfP](https://lists.fosdem.org/pipermail/fosdem/2020q4/003114.html),
  [2022 CfP](https://lists.fosdem.org/pipermail/fosdem/2021q4/003346.html)), except 2024, when the
  CfP dropped Q&A padding ("25min of presentation, please do not plan for Q&A; we will do the
  padding once the schedule is completed",
  [2024 CfP](https://lists.fosdem.org/pipermail/fosdem/2023q4/003508.html)), and 2025/2026, back to
  25 minutes "including Q&A, if any"
  ([2025 CfP](https://lists.fosdem.org/pipermail/fosdem/2024q4/003564.html),
  [2026 CfP](https://lists.fosdem.org/pipermail/fosdem/2025q4/003652.html)).

## Behind-the-scenes practices

### Review and scheduling

- The devroom CfPs say "we are looking for multiple Pythonistas to help us shape the devroom
  schedule", i.e. the schedule is community shaped rather than a single committee
  ([2020 CfP](https://lists.fosdem.org/pipermail/fosdem/2019q4/002885.html)).
- For 2013 the CfP explicitly lists a review committee of four named reviewers
  ([pycon.blogspot.com](https://pycon.blogspot.com/2012/12/cfp-for-python-room-at-fosdem-2013.html)).
- Reviewing is done after the deadline, with the organizers emailing speakers on the notification
  dates listed above; the Pentabarf/Pretalx record is what the organizers schedule from
  ([2021 CfP](https://lists.fosdem.org/pipermail/fosdem/2020q4/003114.html),
  [2025 CfP](https://lists.fosdem.org/pipermail/fosdem/2024q4/003564.html)).
- Speakers must confirm they can attend in person and only then are considered
  ([2025 CfP](https://lists.fosdem.org/pipermail/fosdem/2024q4/003564.html)). The organizers state
  they are not able to offer travel funding; 2019/2020 CfPs alternatively invite presenters
  traveling from outside Europe to ask FOSDEM's own organizers for financial aid
  ([2019 CfP](https://lists.fosdem.org/pipermail/fosdem/2018q4/002781.html)).

### Recordings and licensing

- Since the 2015 CfP, sending a proposal "implies giving permission to be recorded"
  ([2015 CfP](https://lists.fosdem.org/pipermail/fosdem/2014-November/002067.html)).
- From 2019 on, talks are streamed live "under the FOSDEM content licence" and later published in
  the [FOSDEM video archive](https://video.fosdem.org/)
  ([2020 CfP](https://lists.fosdem.org/pipermail/fosdem/2019q4/002885.html)).
- For 2025 and 2026 recordings are released under CC-BY-SA or CC-BY
  ([2025 CfP](https://lists.fosdem.org/pipermail/fosdem/2024q4/003564.html),
  [2026 CfP](https://lists.fosdem.org/pipermail/fosdem/2025q4/003652.html)).
- 2021 and 2022 online editions required all talks to be pre-recorded before the event ("a hard
  requirement from the FOSDEM organizers"), with live Q&A afterwards
  ([2021 CfP](https://lists.fosdem.org/pipermail/fosdem/2020q4/003114.html),
  [2022 CfP](https://lists.fosdem.org/pipermail/fosdem/2021q4/003346.html)).
- The 2013 Python devroom is not in the FOSDEM video archive — no Python folder exists on
  `video.fosdem.org/2013/`, and the room's README documents recording problems that year
  ([video.fosdem.org](https://video.fosdem.org/2013/); also noted in the repo's
  `early-research/fosdem-data-sources.md` research notes).

### Social and volunteer side

- Each edition has had an accompanying Python community dinner ("the tradition to crash somewhere
  to eat and drink while discussing Python things"), booked in advance with details on the mailing
  list and website ([2019 CfP](https://lists.fosdem.org/pipermail/fosdem/2018q4/002781.html)).
- Volunteers are called for in every recent year to help run the room
  ([2024 CfP](https://lists.fosdem.org/pipermail/fosdem/2023q4/003508.html)).
- A community t-shirt and an "AFPyro" restaurant evening were organized in 2014
  ([2014 CfP](https://www.mail-archive.com/python-list@python.org/msg365462.html)).

## Open gaps

- **2018 CfP unverified** — no Python devroom CfP was found on the FOSDEM mailing list (searched
  the 2018q4 index: only unrelated devrooms plus a "Any Python dev room this year?" question, answered by
  pointing to [FOSDEM's accepted-devrooms news](https://lists.fosdem.org/pipermail/fosdem/2018q4/002762.html)), and
  no 2018 snapshot of python-fosdem.org exists in the
  [Wayback Machine](https://web.archive.org/cdx/search/cdx?url=python-fosdem.org&matchType=domain) captures
  (nothing between May 2017 and December 2019). Organizer names and deadlines for FOSDEM 2018
  therefore remain unverified.
- **2018 organizer continuity** — the 2019 CfP is signed by Eric Gazoni and Jens Roelant and opens
  with "we will be running the Python devroom _again_", implying they also ran the 2018 edition,
  but this is not stated outright, so 2018 is left unverified.
- The `python-devroom@lists.fosdem.org` list referenced by the 2015–2017 CfPs has no publicly
  browsable archive ([404](https://lists.fosdem.org/pipermail/python-devroom/)); its contents
  (review discussions, schedules) are lost to public view.
- Attendance figures quoted in the CfPs ("over 8000/8500 participants") are FOSDEM-wide boilerplate
  copied between years, not per-devroom attendance counts.
- No organizer wrap-up blog posts were located for any year; if one exists it would be the best
  additional behind-the-scenes source.
