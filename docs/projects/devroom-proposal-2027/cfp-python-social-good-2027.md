# Call for Participation

This year, we’re excited to focus on the theme of **Python for social good** for our devroom at [FOSDEM 2027](https://www.fosdem.org/), January 30th and 31st 2027. This devroom is one of many tracks in a much bigger event, with a lot of interesting topics. With thousands of participants, FOSDEM is a great place to put your ideas forward and create discussions in our community.

**We’re now looking for talk proposals**, within this theme of Python for social good.

## Guidance for proposals

This year’s theme is **Python for social good**: we want Python projects / people / stories in service of a positive purpose for people, society, and the planet. We’re looking for presentations that further community discussions, with a mixture of case studies, short tutorials, demos, live coding, and lightning talks. We want presentations that:

- Showcase the positive impact of Python.
- Motivate others to contribute to socially beneficial Python projects.
- Demonstrate our community values (openness, inclusivity, collaboration, learner mindset)

### Suggested topics

Here are a few examples of what we are after:

- Civic tech and public sector services
- Accessibility and inclusive design in Python tools and documentation
- Climate, energy, and environmental projects
- Community infrastructure
- Humanitarian, health, and nonprofit applications
- Diversity and inclusion as engineering practice

As with past years, we also love to receive proposals about different aspects of Python:

- The language itself
- Its ecosystem
- Specific usage scenarios

The code behind these projects can be anything: web development (Django, FastAPI, etc), infrastructure and automation, developer tools, data engineering and machine learning, scientific Python, or embedded Python.

### Bonus points

We particularly welcome proposals that:

- Will further discussions within the community, at FOSDEM and beyond.
- Offer a nuanced perspective on the place of AI in technology.
- Come from people who are new to public speaking or open source generally.
- Come from people who are under-represented in tech (diverse backgrounds, genders, ethnicities, etc.).
- Are clearly written by you with your ideas, and not your agent on your behalf.
