# 2027 FOSDEM plans

## Goals

- Organize a great Python presence at FOSDEM 2027, in the continuity of community efforts in past years.
- Respond to FOSDEM organizers’ plans to de-emphasize mainstream devrooms / devrooms that also have official separate events.
- Streamline the organization of Python community efforts at FOSDEM. Being more mindful of our resources, and better using opportunities to collaborate with others.
